Privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026 · Applies to the MnemoScript apps for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android

MnemoScript is a writing app made by ehitimum. It is built to keep your writing on your own device. There is no MnemoScript account and no MnemoScript server: we (the developer) do not receive, store or see your projects, your Google data or anything you type.

What stays on your device

Your projects (chapters, notes, mind maps, fantasy maps, images, AI conversations and backups) are saved as files on your device. Settings are stored on your device. Passwords and access tokens for services you connect (Google, AI providers) are kept in your operating system’s secure credential store.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose Settings → Sync with Google Drive → Connect, you sign in with Google and allow MnemoScript the single permission drive.appdata: access to a hidden folder in your Google Drive that only MnemoScript can see. MnemoScript cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

  • What is stored there: copies of the projects you choose to sync (their text, structure and images) and the records needed to merge changes between your devices.
  • What it is used for: only to keep those projects in step between your own devices. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling or to train AI models, and it is not sold or shared with anyone.
  • Who can access it: you, through MnemoScript on your devices. The data travels directly between your device and Google over an encrypted connection; it never passes through a server of ours. The developer has no access to it.
  • Google account details: MnemoScript reads the connected account’s name, email address and Google account ID, kept on your device, only to show which account is connected and to stop a project being mixed into a different account by mistake.
  • Turning it off: switch sync off for a project, or choose Disconnect in Settings. You can also remove MnemoScript’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the hidden app data in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps.
MnemoScript’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Spelling and grammar hints

When spelling & grammar highlights are on, the text of the chapter you are editing is sent to the LanguageTool service (api.languagetool.org) to be checked. You can turn this off in Settings → Writing. See LanguageTool’s privacy policy.

AI assistant (optional)

If you connect an AI provider (for example Claude or ChatGPT, through your own subscription or API key) and use the assistant or an inline AI command, the text needed for that request is sent to the provider you chose, under that provider’s terms. Nothing is sent to an AI provider unless you connect one and use it.

Phone pairing (optional)

If you pair your phone with your PC, they talk directly over your local network with end-to-end encryption. Nothing goes through the internet or a server of ours.

Children

MnemoScript is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

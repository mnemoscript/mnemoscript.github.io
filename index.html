A calm place to write your book.

MnemoScript is a writing studio for novelists and worldbuilders. Chapters, notes, mind maps and fantasy maps live together in one project, stored as plain folders on your own device.

Download for Windows and Android Source code

Writing that feels smooth

A focused editor with a gliding caret, “/” blocks and formatting, smart quotes and gentle spelling and grammar hints.

Worldbuilding

Mind maps for plots and characters, and a fantasy map studio for drawing your world.

From draft to book

Order your chapters and compile them into a print-ready PDF with a cover and table of contents.

Read aloud & dictation

Hear your prose read back, or dictate new scenes. Speech runs on your device.

Optional AI assistant

Polish, proofread or brainstorm with your own Claude or ChatGPT plan or API key. Off unless you connect it.

Optional Google Drive sync

Keep a project in step between your PC and phone through a private, hidden folder in your own Google Drive.

Your writing stays yours

MnemoScript has no account and no server. Projects are saved on your device. Nothing leaves it unless you turn on a feature that needs to, such as Google Drive sync or the AI assistant, and then it goes straight to the service you chose. The privacy policy explains exactly what is sent where.
